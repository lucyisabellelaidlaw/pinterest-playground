2. Information accessed

When you authorize the Application to connect to your Pinterest account, the Application may access information provided by Pinterest through the Pinterest API, including:

  • Pinterest account information necessary to identify the connected account;
  • Pinterest boards and board information;
  • Pins created or managed through the Application;
  • Pin performance and analytics information, such as impressions, saves, Pin clicks, and outbound clicks.

The Application only requests and accesses Pinterest data that is necessary for its functionality and that has been authorized by the Pinterest account owner.

Information accessed through the Pinterest API is used solely to:

Information obtained through Pinterest is not sold, rented, or used for advertising purposes.

4. Storage and security

The Application may store authentication credentials, such as OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens, in order to maintain an authorized connection to Pinterest.

Authentication credentials are treated as confidential and are not intentionally shared with third parties.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect authentication credentials and other information accessed by the Application from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

5. Sharing of information

The Application does not sell or share Pinterest account information with third parties except where necessary to operate the Application or where required by law.

The Application communicates with Pinterest through the official Pinterest API. Information transmitted to Pinterest is subject to Pinterest's own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data retention

Pinterest data accessed by the Application is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the Application and perform its intended functions.

The account owner may revoke the Application's access to their Pinterest account at any time through Pinterest.

Upon revocation of access, the Application will no longer be able to access the Pinterest account through the revoked authorization.
